Fahrenheit 451, by Ray Bradbury

Fahrenheit 451 is a science fiction novel about a future society where books are banned and firefighters are responsible for burning them. The story follows Guy Montag, a firefighter who begins questioning his work and the society around him. The novel explores censorship, independent thinking, knowledge, technology, and the importance of reading and questioning accepted ideas.

The Hobbit, by J.R.R. Tolkien

The Hobbit follows Bilbo Baggins, a peaceful hobbit who unexpectedly joins a group of dwarves and the wizard Gandalf on an exciting journey. Their goal is to reclaim the dwarves' homeland and treasure from the dragon Smaug. Along the way, Bilbo faces many dangers and discovers courage, intelligence, and abilities that he never knew he possessed.

The Book Thief, by Markus Zusak

The Book Thief tells the story of Liesel Meminger, a young girl living in Nazi Germany during World War II. Liesel develops a strong love for books and begins stealing them while experiencing the difficulties of war. The novel explores friendship, family, courage, loss, and the power of words. It presents an emotional story about finding hope during a difficult and dangerous period in history.
